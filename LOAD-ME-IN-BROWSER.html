How this works

This shows what your page will look like on northstategives.org, using the site's real stylesheets and layout. Nothing here touches the live page.

  1. Story — paste HTML into the text box, or click Open HTML file…. The preview updates as you type. When it's right, click Copy HTML, open your page on northstategives.org, click the <> (code view) button in the editor, replace everything, and save.
  2. Banner, logo, name, colors, goal — change them under Page settings. These are not part of the story HTML: on Mightycause you set them in the page's settings and upload the images there. The preview crops images the same way Mightycause does (banner 3:1, logo 1:1) so you can see what will be cut off.
  3. Use Desktop / Tablet / Phone. Most Giving Tuesday visitors are on phones.
  4. Warnings under the text box flag things the Mightycause editor will strip or that will look wrong on phones.

Everything you change is remembered in this browser. Reset to live page puts back the current live banner, logo, name and colors. The Mightycause editor may still remove a few things (it has its own cleanup rules), so look at the live page once after saving.
